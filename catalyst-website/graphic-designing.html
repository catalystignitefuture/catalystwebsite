Frequently asked

Graphic design FAQs.

A few common questions before you start your next design project.

How much does graphic designing cost?

Our listed starting price is AED 10 per design. The final price may vary depending on complexity, content, dimensions, quantity, revisions, and other requirements.

Can you use my existing logo and branding?

Yes. You can provide your existing logo, colours, fonts, brand guidelines, images, and other relevant materials when discussing the project.

Can you create social media designs?

Yes. Social media posts, promotional graphics, announcements, and other digital visuals can be discussed as part of your graphic design requirements.

Can I request a custom design?

Absolutely. Send us your idea, reference examples, required content, preferred dimensions, and intended use so the project can be discussed properly.

What file format will I receive?

The appropriate delivery format depends on how the design will be used. Discuss your intended use with us so the required output format can be agreed before work begins.

How do I start a design project?

Use the WhatsApp enquiry button and tell us what you need, what the design is for, and any references or materials you already have.
