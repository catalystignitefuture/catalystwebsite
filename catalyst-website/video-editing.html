FAQ

Questions before you start?

What is included in the AED 45–60 price?

The price covers a standard video editing project. The exact scope depends on the footage, video length, complexity, effects, captions and other requirements.

Can you edit Instagram Reels and TikTok videos?

Yes. We can prepare vertical short-form videos suitable for social media platforms, depending on the requirements of the project.

Do I need to provide the footage?

In most projects, you provide the original footage and any important brand assets, references or instructions. We then handle the editing process.

Can you add text and captions?

Yes. Text, titles, captions and other visual elements can be included when required. Requirements may affect the final price.

Is every video the same price?

No. AED 45–60 is the starting price range for video editing. Larger or more complex projects may require a customized quote.
