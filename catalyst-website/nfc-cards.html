Questions answered

Frequently asked questions.

Here are a few things to know before ordering your NFC business cards.

What is an NFC business card?

An NFC business card contains a small near-field communication chip. When tapped against a compatible phone, it can open a programmed destination, such as a digital profile or website.

Does the recipient need to install an app?

A recipient generally does not need a dedicated app to open a standard web link. Phone compatibility, NFC settings, and the placement of the card can affect how it works.

Will it work with iPhone and Android?

Compatible iPhone and Android devices can read NFC tags. Exact compatibility and the way the tag is detected can vary by device model and settings.

What can I link to my NFC card?

You can discuss linking your card to a business profile, website, portfolio, social media page, digital menu, or another suitable online destination with the CATALYST team.
